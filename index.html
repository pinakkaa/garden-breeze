Overview

GARDEN BREEZE @ THE PRESTIGE CITY

At Sarjapur, Bangalore

A world where life is full of possibilities. Spread over 200+ acres, The Prestige City is a self-contained community of high-rise towers, plots & villas, which includes multiple clubhouses, a Forum Mall and acres of sprawling gardens. Brilliantly planned, The Prestige City offers you the opportunity to experience community living at its very best
Introducing Garden Breeze is a premium residential development within The Prestige City, thoughtfully planned over 10 acres, redefining urban living amidst nature.The master plan comprises four elegant towers, positioned in a meticulously landscaped garden setting that frames the adjoining lake. A collection of thoughtfully planned residences that balance form and function with contextual elegance. Each home has been designed to enhance natural light, cross ventilation, and spatial fluidity, creating interiors that feel both open and grounded. Spanning over 10 acres with 90% open spaces, the development is designed to encourage social interaction and moments of quiet retreat, each space serves a purpose beyond mere function.
